Generational Heritage

Born in
Baghdad.
Crafted for
royalty.

Since 1954 along the historic banks of the Tigris in Al-Karrada, Baghdadia has guarded the sacred formulas of Mesopotamian pastry-making.

While industrial patisseries turned to hydrogenated vegetable shortening and artificial flavorings, our hearths remain steadfast. We use only 100% clarified sheep ghee (دهن حر) from northern pastures, hand-pitted Zahdi dates from Karbala orchards, green cardamom pods crushed fresh at dawn, and water-buffalo ashta sourced daily.

Every tray of Daheen, every diamond of Samoon, and every golden crescent of Kleicha carries the fragrance of a seventy-year tradition of Iraqi hospitality (كرم الضيافة البغدادي).

Our Unbroken Rules

Five pillars of uncompromising quality.

Pure Iraqi Dihin Hor
Zero margarine, palm oils, or hydrogenated substitutes. Only grass-fed clarified sheep butter for incomparable aroma.
01
450°C Refractory Ovens
Stone hearths and clay tannour walls that impart authentic blistered crackle and wood-fired character to every loaf.
02
Karbala Zahdi Dates
Single-origin dates slow-simmered with cardamom seeds and toasted sesame, retaining deep caramel undertones.
03
Twice-Daily Fresh Batches
Baking shifts at 6:00 AM and 4:00 PM. No day-old pastries or chilled shelf inventory. Fresh from the flame.
