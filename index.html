Our story

Born from
Kurdistan.
Made for the world.

We are a Kurdish heritage clothing brand based in Erbil, Kurdistan Region. Every piece we sell tells a story — a story of mountains, colors, and generations of craftsmanship.

We believe traditional clothing shouldn't cost a fortune. Our mission is to make authentic Kurdish fashion accessible to Kurds everywhere, from Erbil to Berlin to Los Angeles.
