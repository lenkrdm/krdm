— OUR STORY

Born from
Baghdad.
Made for
the world.

We are an Iraqi traditional bakery and sweets house rooted in Baghdad. Every bake tells a story — a story of clay ovens, fragrant cardamom, and generations of craftsmanship.

We believe traditional sweets shouldn't compromise on real ingredients. We use 100% clarified sheep ghee (دهن حر), single-origin Karbala Zahdi dates, and fresh water-buffalo ashta.
